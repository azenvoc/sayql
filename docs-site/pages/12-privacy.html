The Ask box never sends rows from your tables to an AI provider. Investigate can, only on connections where you turn it on; it is off by default. With a local model, nothing leaves your computer.

Over MCP, counts and summaries (the default) and masked rows keep your rows from reaching an AI app by accident, but a count or a sum is still a fact about your rows, and an agent set on it can turn a value into one. Columns marked never send are the firm line: SayQL refuses any query that could read them.

See it at a glance

The status bar shows, for the connection you're on, the most that can reach an AI from it: No rows to AI, Counts to AI, Masked rows to AI or All rows to AI apps. Its tooltip has a line each for the Ask box, Investigate, AI apps and never-send columns; click it to change them. Right-click a connection and choose Overview… for the same, plus what AI apps did with it, when it was last audited, the knowledge sources its audit reads, and your recent queries on it.

Your activity

Settings → Your activity lists the queries you ran in SayQL: from the editor, the Ask box and Investigate, with when, the connection, the SQL, how many rows came back and how long it took. For Investigate it also says what went to the AI for each query. It's kept on this computer (in SayQL's settings folder), holds the SQL and counts, never the rows, and keeps roughly the last 2 MB. Clear the log deletes it. A connection's overview shows its part of the log. What AI apps ran over MCP is in its own log, under Settings → AI apps.

Voice input

When you say your question, the speech model (Whisper) turns it into text on your computer. Audio stays in memory only while you talk, is never saved, and is never sent anywhere, not even to your AI provider. Only the text goes on, like a typed question. Remove the model in Settings → Voice.

Feedback and the website

Feedback in the status bar opens a short form, then a pre-filled public GitHub issue in your browser. Your text is filled in, plus your SayQL version and operating system for feedback and bug reports. Nothing is sent until you post it.
